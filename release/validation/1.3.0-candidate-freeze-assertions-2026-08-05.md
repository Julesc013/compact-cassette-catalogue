## Final source closure

After every selected Candidate lane finishes, the build entry point repeats:

- `HEAD` still equals `C`;
- the worktree is clean and submodules are exact;
- the expected remote snapshot still equals `C` and the provider receipt;
- genome and lane projections still pass; and
- the external lock still has its original SHA-256.

creation requires that record and binds it to the same package-set source and
lock. This is in addition to each lane's precompile source checks and final
tool/lock stability checks.
