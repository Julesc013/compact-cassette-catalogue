## Remediation during Alpha qualification

- Removed two projected settings-fixture links to 2.x-only preference documents
  and replaced them with the 1.3 settings plan and transition matrix.
- Changed launch smoke from hidden to minimized windows because hidden WinForms
  processes do not expose a reliable `MainWindowHandle`.
- Increased the per-lane startup bound to 30 seconds and added inter-lane cleanup
  after one transient net481 handle timeout. The isolated retry and complete
  four-lane rerun passed.

No application behaviour was changed by these remediations.
