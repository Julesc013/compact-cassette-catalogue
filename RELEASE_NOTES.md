# Compact Cassette Catalogue 2.0.0 Alpha 2

C3 2.0.0 Alpha 2 is an in-development checkpoint in the repository-owned 2.0
release train. Its scope, evidence, limitations, and package identities will be
recorded here before candidate freeze.

Alpha 2 turns the broad 1.x compatibility goal into a bounded laboratory. Its
schema-validated corpus inventories all 10 public 1.x tags, exact source commits,
11 official assets, three settings profiles, five update behaviors, and four
observed catalogue formats. Stable 1.x producers plus the maintained 1.2 Beta 1
are the exact support baseline; historical prereleases are inventory-only.

Hash-pinned official binaries complete six automated
current-writer/old-reader/old-writer/current-reader rows. Privacy-safe official-
writer fixtures load through the secure production adapter, settings migration
covers absent/malformed/repeated/transient cases, and protocol tests dry-run
update and promotion boundaries. Behavior is classified as required, tolerated,
or corrected rather than preserving historical defects accidentally.

The product still writes catalogue format 1.1.0. Alpha 2 does not introduce the
native-v2 format, migrate catalogues, or silently replace any legacy source.
Formats 1.0.0 through 1.0.2 are evidenced as pre-1.x archival formats and remain
outside the 1.x promise unless a later explicit import profile is accepted.

This checkpoint is not published. See the
[execution plan](docs/planning/2.0-execution-plan.md) and
[validation record](release/validation/2.0.0-alpha.2.md).
