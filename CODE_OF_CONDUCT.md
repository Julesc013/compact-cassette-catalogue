# Community Code of Conduct

C3 welcomes respectful participation from users and contributors of all
backgrounds and experience levels.

Be constructive, specific, and patient. Discuss the work rather than attacking
people. Do not harass, threaten, demean, discriminate against, or publish private
information about another participant. Assume mistakes are possible, but do not
use that assumption to dismiss repeated harmful conduct.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Julesc013). Do not include sensitive details
in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as practical.
